1. **Capacity Expansion** (40-50% of capex)

   - Quality improvement projects

- **Geographic Focus:** Northeast, Bihar, South India seeing maximum investment

## 🎯 KEY INSIGHTS & TRENDS

### Revenue Growth Strategy
1. **Volume-Led Growth:** Most companies prioritizing volume expansion over pricing power
2. **Premium Products:** 400 bps increase in premium products for several companies
3. **Market Share Gains:** Adani Cement targeting 20% market share by FY28 (from 15%)
4. **Trade Channel Focus:** Expansion of dealer networks and direct dispatch

### Capex Strategy
1. **Front-Loading Investments:** Heavy capex in FY25-26 to capture demand growth
2. **Brownfield First:** Preference for brownfield expansions for faster commissioning
3. **Subsidy Optimization:** Leveraging state subsidies (SGST benefits in Northeast)
4. **Sustainability Drive:** Mandatory green investments for future compliance

### Risk Factors Mentioned
- **Demand Volatility:** Monsoon, elections, and global uncertainties
- **Input Cost Inflation:** Fuel and raw material price pressures
- **Capacity Utilization:** Industry at 65-75%, need demand growth for profitability
- **Competition Intensity:** New capacity additions may pressure pricing
